Works offline

Add expenses and income, repeat monthly entries and read reports with no connection.

Attach receipts

Snap a photo or attach a file to any entry, so the bill is next to the number.

Reports and charts

See yearly summaries, a category pie chart and a next-month forecast, and export reports as PDF or Excel for any date range.

Back up your way

Save to a JSON file, sync to your own Google Drive, or export to Excel. You choose where the copy goes.

Lock and themes

Protect the app with a 4-digit password and pick Light, Dark, Sand or follow your device.

Remove ads

Watch a short ad for a few ad-free days, or choose a Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime plan through Google Play.

Your entries stay on your phone.

There are no servers and no sign-up. If you turn on Drive backup, the app saves its backup to a private app-data folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can access, not the rest of your Drive.
