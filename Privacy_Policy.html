1Who we are

Expense Tracker ("the app") is developed by Syed Mohsin (Jasin Creations). Jasin Creations is a trade name. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who else is involved, and what choices you have. If you have any question, see Contact.

2Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your phone, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your expense and income entries (date, category or source, amount, hours or days worked, notes, recurring flag).
  • Attachments: if you attach a receipt photo or file to an entry, a copy of it (images are resized and compressed) is stored inside the app together with that entry.
  • Your categories, currency symbol, language and theme.
  • If you enable it, a one-way hash of your 4-digit password (the password itself is not stored).
  • The remaining ad-free time you earned by watching an ad.
  • The last verified purchase status (for example "Monthly"), so Premium keeps working briefly if you are offline.
  • Time readings from your device (uptime clock and boot counter) used only to count down ad-free time and the offline Premium grace period. This information stays on your device.
  • If you connect Google Drive, the email address of the Google account you chose and the ID of the backup file.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to back it up or export it (see section 4).

3What we do not collect

  • We do not collect, see, store or sell your financial entries, attachments or any personal information.
  • We have no user accounts, no login and no servers that receive your data.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. Third-party services listed in section 7 may collect limited technical data as described there.
  • We do not access your contacts, location or microphone.
  • The app uses your camera, photos or files only when you tap "Attach receipt or file", and only for the single photo or file you take or pick. We never scan or browse your gallery.

4Backup, export and import

All of these are optional and started by you:

  • Backup file: you choose a location with the system "save as" screen. The app then rewrites a file (Expense_Backup.json) after every change. The file contains all your entries and settings, including any attached receipts and files, and the password hash if you enabled a password.
  • Google Drive: if you connect Drive, the app saves the same file to your own Google Drive using the limited drive.file permission. This lets the app see only files it created itself, not the rest of your Drive. If a backup already exists there, the app reads it so you can choose to load it. The file goes between your phone and your Google account; it does not pass through any server of ours.
  • Excel and JSON: exporting creates a file where you choose (the Excel export contains the entry rows only, not attachments). Importing reads a file you pick.
  • Saving an attachment: if you tap "Download file" on an attachment, a copy is saved to your phone's Downloads folder.
  • Android system backup: the app allows Android's built-in backup. Depending on your phone settings, Android may back up app data to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is controlled by Google and your device settings.

Anyone who can open your backup or export file can read your entries and attachments. Keep those files private.

Google user data. If you connect Google Drive, the app receives your Google account email and access to the single backup file it creates. It uses them only to save, update and read that file for you. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not read by people. It stays between your phone and your Google account.

Expense Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banner ads, a native ad in the menu and settings pages, and optional rewarded video ads. Rewarded ads give you ad-free days or let you continue an action such as exporting, importing, changing the theme or resetting.

  • AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where required, the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. Where you decline, ads are not personalised or not shown as the law requires.
  • Change your mind: where applicable, open Menu → Settings → Ad consent to review or withdraw your choice at any time.
  • Device controls: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can remove ads with a purchase (section 6).

More information: How Google uses data in ads · Google Privacy Policy.

6Purchases (remove ads)

You can remove ads with a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details.

  • The app asks Google Play, on your device, whether your Google account owns a purchase. This is how Premium is restored on a new device signed in with the same account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play (Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play according to its policies. If a subscription ends or is refunded, ads return.
  • Premium is not stored in backups and cannot be transferred by importing a file.

9Security

Your data lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. The optional password locks the app screen, but it is a convenience lock, not strong encryption of the stored data. Backup and export files, and attachments you download, are not encrypted, so store them safely. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Retention and deletion

  • On your device: data, including attachments, stays until you delete it. Delete a single entry to remove its attachment, or use Menu → Backup and Reset → Reset all data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Temporary photos taken with the camera are kept in the app's own folder and are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall.
  • Backups: files you saved or exported, attachments you downloaded, and Expense_Backup.json in your Google Drive, remain until you delete them yourself. Android system backups can be managed in your Google account settings.
  • Disconnecting Drive: choose "Turn off auto-save" in the app and, if you wish, revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Data held by Google (ads, purchases) is kept according to Google's policies.

11Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You keep full control through the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law) you may also have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and ask for a copy;
  • withdraw consent at any time (see section 5), without affecting earlier processing;
  • object to or limit certain processing, and complain to your local data protection authority.

For data processed by Google (such as ad personalisation), you can use your Google account's Data & privacy controls. We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the ad services described above. Where the law requires a legal basis, ads personalisation relies on your consent and purchases rely on contract performance.

12Children

The app is a general-audience finance tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

13International data transfers

Google may process ad, purchase and Drive data on servers in different countries, under its own safeguards. Your entries and attachments do not leave your device unless you back them up or export them.
